E-invite studio launch brief

The demo is the shop window. All three sites sell from live demo links shared on WhatsApp and Instagram. Invifest watermarks its demos and puts a "Buy now for yourself" button inside them.

The first tap is the product. Wax-seal envelopes, velvet curtains and pearl curtains open on a tap, before any wedding details appear. That opening is what guests remember and forward.

There's a gap in the middle. Invifest sells Indian templates at ₹2,499. The Digital Yes sells art-directed European designs from ₹19,377 to ₹1,07,960. Nobody sells that level of art for Haldi-to-Reception weddings at an Indian price.

Art matters more than code. None of the three use 3D or video for the opening. They use layered illustrations with smooth animation, so our demos win on illustration, type and timing.

The three sites

invifest

A Europe-based studio selling in 35+ countries through Instagram and TikTok. It sells on speed and unlimited changes, for weddings and every other event, from birthdays to corporate parties.

What's trending

Ten patterns that show up across the sites. Each one is a design decision we should make on purpose, not copy.

A tap-to-open first screen

An envelope with a wax seal, a velvet curtain or a pearl curtain hides the invitation until the guest taps. "Tap to reveal" is the whole first screen.

invifestDigital YesWebgency

Painted covers

Watercolour couples seen from behind, painted venues and garden scenes. The Digital Yes charges ₹4,983 extra for a venue illustration.

invifest BansuriDigital Yes

Initials as a crest

The couple's initials on the wax seal and as a monogram, such as invifest's gold "AM" with a peacock feather and flute. The Digital Yes sells a custom monogram for ₹2,768.

invifestDigital Yes

Gold script names, spaced capitals

Names in flowing gold script, small labels in widely spaced capitals ("MARK YOUR CALENDAR"), and details in a classic serif. Cormorant Garamond appears on two of the three sites.

invifestDigital Yes

Drifting petals and music

Rose and marigold petals drift over every section, and a round button in the corner mutes the music. It's quiet motion that never stops.

invifestWebgency

Every ceremony on one link

Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Baraat, Pheras and Reception each get a card, with a countdown and map. Family blessings name late grandparents, as a printed card would.

invifest

RSVP that feeds a dashboard

Replies land in a guest list, not an inbox: a private dashboard with personalised guest links and a seating plan, or at least a Google Sheet and a QR code.

Digital YesWebgency

Playful reveals

Scratch the card to reveal the date, scratch-off photo save-the-dates, and a design that turns from day to night as you scroll.

Webgency Dolce VitaDigital Yes

Collections by community

Separate ranges for Hindu, South Indian, Muslim, Sikh and Christian weddings, each with its own words and symbols, and regional languages next to English.

Where we fit

Art direction as rich as The Digital Yes, built around Indian ceremonies, at an Indian price and delivered in 48 hours.

  • We've already shipped two.Your RSVP invite (diptanshunidhi.vercel.app) and the Mandir Swarn family invite (diptanshuwedsnidhi.vercel.app) already have RSVP to Google Sheets, looping music, a Hindi switch and WhatsApp share images.
  • One engine, many designs.If every demo reads the couple's details from one file, a new order is a form to fill, not a rebuild. That's how we beat invifest's 3 days without a 15-a-month cap.
  • Every demo sells itself.A sample watermark and a "Get this design" WhatsApp button in each demo, so Instagram visitors land on the product itself.

Skills to install

This PC has no Claude skills or plugins installed yet, and no Node.js or Git, which three of the installs below need. Start with those two.

Node.js (LTS)

From nodejs.org. Needed by the GSAP, Remotion and Playwright installs, and to preview the reels.

Git for Windows

From git-scm.com. Needed to add skills from GitHub and to send demos to Vercel without dragging files into GitHub by hand.

frontend-design

Anthropic · official plugin

Makes Claude commit to a distinct palette, font pairing and motion for each page, so ten demos don't come out looking like one template in ten colours.

/plugin install frontend-design@claude-plugins-official

GSAP skills

GreenSock · official

Correct, current code for the GSAP animation library: timelines for envelopes, shutters, curtains and doors, reveals as you scroll, and petal loops that stay smooth on mid-range Android phones.

/plugin marketplace add greensock/gsap-skills

Example skills

Anthropic · 4 skills we'd use

canvas-design for posters and share images. algorithmic-art for generated kolam, alpona, rangoli and petal fields. theme-factory to fix one colour-and-font theme per demo. webapp-testing for automatic phone-size checks.

/plugin marketplace add anthropics/skills /plugin install example-skills@anthropic-agent-skills

Playwright MCP

Microsoft · browser control

Lets Claude open each demo at phone size, tap through the opening, and save screenshots and screen recordings. Chrome froze on the animated demos today, and it froze on your own invites before.

claude mcp add playwright npx @playwright/mcp@latest

Remotion skills

Remotion · official

Builds vertical 9:16 reels of each demo from code, swapping names, dates and music per video, for Instagram and WhatsApp status. Run it inside a Remotion project folder.

npx skills add remotion-dev/skills

Already here, nothing to install

  • Design canvas, for laying out mood boards and demo screens
  • Artifacts, for private preview links like this page
  • Adobe connector: font search, background removal, image cut-outs, video resizing
  • Figma connector
  • Canva connector, once you sign in to it in claude.ai connector settings

Outside Claude

  • Painted art. Claude draws line art and patterns, like the Ganesha in your family invite, but not watercolour paintings. Painted couples and venues need an image generator such as Adobe Firefly, Midjourney or ChatGPT images, on a plan that allows commercial use.
  • Music. Pixabay, as used for your family invite: free for commercial use, no credit needed.
  • Hosting. The GitHub and Vercel accounts you already have.

  • English plus one regional language
