The creative system in ten answers

What Mogra & Moti is

Recognisable by
The strand — one champagne thread of alternating pearls and closed mogra buds, lying across cotton paper in raking morning light — and two tall names held apart by that thread.
Signature opening
The light finds their names. Light rakes across a blind-debossed card; a pearl rolls in and stops; its thread pulls taut; the strand is strung bead by bead; the names are uncovered by the same light. Touch the pearl and the thread is pulled away.
Signature material
Nacre, the lustre of pearl: a soft travelling highlight with blush and sage overtones. It replaces gold as the luxury cue and appears only in highlights.
Signature transition
The Draw. A thread tightens and draws the next scene up like a new sheet of paper, beads riding along. Used three times: into Celebrations, into the reply, into the closing knot.
Signature type
No ampersand. Rohan high and left, Anaya low and right, in condensed Imbue at masthead scale, with the thread running through the gap. Three appearances only.
Signature image
Graded to the hour, bleeding off one edge, settling into place. Every photo matches its chapter's light, touches one screen edge (bar a few declared full-screen moments), and opens from that edge.
Indian, not "Indian wedding"
Mogra strung as buds that bloom at night, the mangalsutra's thread, the gathbandhan knot, chikankari shadow work, real henna colour, Devanagari chapter numerals, santoor. No mandalas, paisleys, red-and-gold or palace silhouettes.
Premium, not template
A composition per scene, one consistent light, big scale contrast, three elements per screen, choreography with causes, written copy, no cards, pills or icons, and a first screen that holds 60 fps.
Hierarchy at 390 px
Moment → fact → action → detail. One image or the names, one large fact, one action in the thumb zone, then small grouped details. Two tiny fixed controls only.
Remembered
"The light found their names, a pearl rolled across the card, and by the end every flower had opened." The last frame: a knotted strand in full bloom under one lamp.

Materials

White on white, told apart by light

One key light from the top left in every scene. Expensive looks are rendered once in the pipeline; at runtime only position, opacity and small masks move.

Nacre

The signature. Photographed pearls, a highlight that never rotates, blush and sage fringes. Replaces gold.

Cotton-rag paper

The invitation itself. Fibres visible only in raking light; the monogram is blind-debossed and appears when light touches it.

Champagne thread

The concept's object. Tension, one tiny release overshoot, beads that bunch then space. Never a divider or a timeline.

Mogra, bud to bloom

Closed at dawn, open at night, following the page's clock. Always photographed, never drawn.

Chanderi, chikankari

Sheer silk-cotton; its embroidery shows only as a soft shadow on the paper beneath.

Lamp light

The only light at night. It makes pearl text and gold legible and the final strand glow.

Anti-patterns

Never in Mogra & Moti

Openings

  • Curtains, envelopes, wax seals, doors, scratch foil
  • A centred hero with names, date and a scroll cue
  • Pulsing "tap to open"

Layout

  • Bordered cards, identical event cards, timelines with dots
  • Countdown tiles
  • A floating gradient "Get this design" pill

Imagery

  • Stock couples smiling at the camera; AI faces
  • Drawn flowers, mandalas, paisleys, palace silhouettes
  • Red-and-gold, HDR, gold gradient fills

Motion

  • Fade-up on scroll, bounce, scale-in, blur-in
  • Petal showers, confetti, sparkles, floating hearts
  • Things that move with no visible cause
